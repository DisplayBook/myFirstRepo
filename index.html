Before you start

This app is a personal exercise helper. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. Always follow guidance from your doctors and physiotherapist, and check with them before starting or changing exercises.

  • Exercise in a cool room, sitting or supported.
  • Nothing should hurt. Stop if it does.
  • Keep pressure off your toes, especially the sore little toe.

Your data stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Let's stop for now

Please stop exercising for now. An elevation break (lying down with your feet above heart level and gentle ankle pumps) is a kinder option. Your next session will be made lighter automatically.

If the redness is spreading, the foot is hot and swollen, or one calf is suddenly painful, swollen, warm or red, get medical help. See warning signs.
